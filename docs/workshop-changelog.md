0.5.9

Dig in whole towns, start mid-war, fight through guerrilla country, and command from a calmer map. Ready for Arma Reforger 1.9 (with Better Battle Buddies 0.1.8).

REFORGER 1.9

- AI vehicles move again. 1.9's automatic gearbox left vehicles that back out of a jam, pull over or back away from enemy armour sitting still. A vehicle its crew leaves now keeps its handbrake on instead of rolling downhill.
- Loads with long mod lists. With ACE, content packs, AiO and more loaded, the game could refuse to load its scripts. Fixed here, in Better Battle Buddies and in AiO.

NEW

- Fortify a whole base. Aim Fortify at the middle of a base you hold and a squad works the town in three tiers: sandbag lines across the enemy's approaches, then MG nests, wire and a checkpoint, then bunkers, towers and a second line. Every town gets its own style of defence, and a reserve runs onto the bags when the enemy comes near.
- Quick start (setup screen): begin at the fighting stage, with the map split along a front line and garrisons, roadblocks, positions and minefields already in place. Even, or one side holding two thirds of the map.
- FIA guerrillas: road ambushes, gunmen in houses, and hideouts that keep them supplied. Optionally FIA picks sides by how each side treats it.
- Company kits: give each side a Rifle, Mechanized, Weapons, Combat engineer or Recon company. The AI's doctrine follows its company.
- Recon flight (15 RP): a jet photographs a 2.4 km strip and puts the enemy it saw on your map. Enemy radio calls can now be overheard, showing a rough ring on the map.
- Second-in-command (host option): an AI 2IC runs the squads and FOBs nobody claims. Ask it for support, hand squads over or take them back, and let it suggest FOB sites.
- New orders: Defend on a road lays an ambush. QRF stands by and hunts any enemy within 300 m. Minefields in any shape, a line or an area. Big squads ride in convoys of two or three vehicles.
- Content packs: optional add-ons can bring era vehicles, soldiers and an attack helicopter support into the game. The Cold War pack is the first.
- More maps: the scenario generator now handles Chernarus Minus, Gulfcoast Islands, Kunar Province, West Zagoria and Zimnitrita (separate add-ons).
- Experimental tab (setup screen): new features to try, all off until switched on, each with a line saying what it does. AI commanders that push down one lane, an AI job planner, an AI that forecasts where it will be attacked and defends ahead of it, AI squads that fall back in covered bounds, period jets over the recon flight and bomb run, AI squad tactics inside bases, vehicles that pull over before a get-out, unarmed vehicles that drive away from armour, guns that clear the bushes in front of them, and an "I'm the 2IC" mode where the AI commander runs the side while you keep command.
- Optional, off by default: big maps get bigger armies.

BETTER

- A calmer command map: dark panels, base dots, one icon for squads at a base, hover cards, a searchable squad list, layer switches, control groups (Ctrl + 1-9), panels you can drag, and a dark map with readable place names. Click an INTEL line to jump to the spot.
- Spotted enemies show what was seen: tank, APC, truck, mortar, MG or AT team, with its size, direction and age.
- 3D camera: down to eye height, Hide UI (H) and Follow a squad (F).
- The ACA panel always fits on screen; build orders and FOB upgrades fold away.
- AI infantry ride on long trips instead of walking for half an hour, keep off open roads in enemy ground, and cross rivers on bridges.
- AI vehicles drive whole trips smoothly instead of stopping at every waypoint, and roadblocks leave a lane trucks can get through.
- Gunners leave a gun that is out of ammo and fight on foot. Held guns carry a bigger ammo reserve.
- Skill and spotting set in Better Battle Buddies' Settings tab now reach AC: Actual's soldiers.

FIXED

- Squads and vehicles no longer spawn inside sand piles, rubble or hangars.
- The arsenal at a FOB's ammo dump works again.
- Squads holding a Fortify position stand behind their sandbags, not in front of them.
- A garrison order no longer stalls because one man is stuck at a door.
- Players who join a server see checkpoints and structures on slopes as the server has them.
- A helicopter crippled in the air really comes down, and one pushed off its pad returns to it.
- The after-action report counts vehicles lost after their crew bailed out.
- Many smaller fixes to AI vehicles, air lifts, FOBs, rides and mission restarts. The full list is in CHANGELOG.md on the mod's GitHub page.
